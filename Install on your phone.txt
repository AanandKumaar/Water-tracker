LITTLE BY LITTLE — INSTALLATION

This is a personal, offline-friendly water tracker. Your goal and drink log stay in this browser on your phone.

FIRST SETUP
1. Put these five app files on a secure (HTTPS) static website, all in the same folder: index.html, water-reminder.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, and icon.svg.
2. Open the site while online. Wait a few seconds for the app to load and cache itself.
3. Android: in Chrome, open the browser menu and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
4. Open the new home-screen app once. It will work offline after the first successful load.

CUSTOM REMINDERS
Turn on reminders, choose an interval (or a custom interval from 5 to 720 minutes), set active start/end times, and enter your own reminder message. Allow notifications if prompted. Reminders run while the app is open; phones may pause them in the background.

DARK MODE
Use the Dark mode button at the top of the app. Your choice is saved on this device.

PRIVACY
Drink entries, preferences, and the daily goal are saved locally in the browser on your device. There is no account or server-side tracking. Clearing browser site data will erase the log.
